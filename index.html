[appendix] faq

Questions, answered

How do I get my download?

Checkout is handled securely by Etsy. Right after you buy, your download link appears, and it's saved under Purchases in your account forever.

Can I use these on my iPad?

Yes. The journals open beautifully in GoodNotes, Notability and other PDF note apps, or print and bind them.

What paper should I print on?

Regular printer paper is perfect for puzzles. For markers and gel pens, try 65 lb cardstock.

Can I print more than once?

Print as many copies as your household (or one classroom or care facility) needs.

How does the personalized journal work?

Add the name and pick your edition at checkout. We make it and send it to your Etsy account within 24 hours.

How do the personalized wedding signs work?

Type your names, date and venue and pick a style at checkout. Bundle orders can upload a guest list for the seating chart. Your print-ready PDFs arrive in your Etsy account within 48 hours, with one free revision. Printing isn't included.

*Really no backwards words?

In our large print Memory Lane books, never. Words run only across, down, or diagonally down, so it stays relaxing.
